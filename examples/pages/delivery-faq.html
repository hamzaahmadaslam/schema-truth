Delivery and returns

How long does delivery take?

Orders arrive in 2 to 4 working days. You get a tracking link by email when your order leaves us.

Can I return shoes I have worn?

Only unworn shoes can be returned, within 30 days of delivery.

Do you ship to Canada?

We ship to most provinces. Delivery to the territories is not available yet.
